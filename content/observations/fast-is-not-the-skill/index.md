When people marvel at high-leverage engineers moving fast, they assume it's typing speed, keyboard shortcuts, or raw brute force.

It almost never is. The fastest builders are fast because they eliminate 90% of the dead ends before writing a single line. Experience is mostly a catalog of traps you know how to step around.
